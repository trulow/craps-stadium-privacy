Craps Stadium – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy covers both apps: Craps Stadium (Dealer) and Craps Stadium (Player). Craps Stadium is a craps table for friends on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. It uses no real money: bankrolls and chips are play money with no cash value, and there are no prizes (see the Terms of Service).

What we collect

We don't collect personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics in either app.

What stays on your devices

  • Games run only on your local network. The dealer's device hosts the table and the players' devices connect to it directly; game traffic is encrypted and never sent to the internet.
  • The display name and starting amount a player enters are sent only to the dealer's device and shown to the other players at that table.
  • The dealer app stores tables, bets and settlement records on the dealer's device so a game can be restored. The dealer can delete them in the app at any time.

Subscriptions (dealer app only)

The optional dealer subscription is sold through Google Play. Google Play handles payment; we never see your payment details. To check whether a subscription is active, the dealer app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous ID created by the app and the Google Play purchase record. This information is used only to unlock subscription features. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices / local network: to find and connect to the dealer's table on your Wi-Fi or hotspot. Android describes this as finding the relative position of nearby devices; Craps Stadium does not use your location.
  • Location (dealer app, Android 12 and older only): Android requires it to start a local hotspot. Your location is not read or stored.
  • Camera (player app): to scan the dealer's QR code. Scanning happens on the device with Google ML Kit; no images are stored or sent. ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic and usage information.
  • Notifications: to show that you're seated at or hosting a table, so you can get back to it from other apps.

Children

Craps Stadium is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
